## Page Intro

Young Adult Grace Groups are the primary setting for close friendships and fellowship in caring for others at Grace Bible Church. Groups meet in homes or comfortable church space and encourage one another in following Christ. Formats are simple; childcare is available. These groups thrive as they care for one another during difficult times — sickness, hospitalization, the death of a loved one — and through the everyday work of walking with Jesus together.

**To join a Grace Group, contact David Hobbs** (Pastor of Discipleship & Pastoral Care): dave@gbcdayton.org.
